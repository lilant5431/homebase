HOMEBASE / PHASE 2.6A / DESIGN STUDY

One work surface. Two atmospheres.

Conceptual desktop and phone compositions, not a working redesign. All data is illustrative. Native Date/Time specimens can open pickers but nothing is saved. Large boards are intentionally scrollable for review. Product reflow and tablet/short-landscape behavior are specified in the documents.

Materials & Motion Showcase

Review-only visual specimens, isolated from Homebase. Buttons expose native hover/focus/press styling but do not navigate, save, or access storage. Fixed state samples and storyboards are labeled. No animation library or ongoing ambient animation.

02 / Menu entry and exit

Initial · closed
Appearance ▾
Intermediate · 80ms
Edge silhouette only; no half-opacity text claim.
Final · 160ms
✓ System — selected
Light · Dark

Enter 160ms standard; exit 120ms cubic-bezier(.4,0,1,1). Desktop menu opacity and at most 8px travel; text and its required baseplate stay fully readable throughout. Closing removes interaction immediately and restores opener focus. Reopening reverses current progress without queued exits. Reduced motion: immediate show/hide. Purpose: connect the menu to its trigger. No mobile editor transform.

120ms standard easing after successful persistence/source adoption. Manual label and lock icon appear synchronously; only a decorative check settles in opacity. Invalid intent, failed save or cancel stays in the original state with its real error; never show temporary success. Reduced motion: immediate final state. No session movement or completion credit. Purpose: distinguish user intent from a recommendation.
