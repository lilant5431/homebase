Both engines: 16 combinations and 352 computed roles each; all 704 sampled/envelope checks pass AA 4.5:1. Frosted remains 72% surface alpha, 4px desktop/3px phone blur, opacity 1 on the text subtree. Reduced effects and unsupported blur use actual opaque panels and preserve the selected material. No new text roles or tokens were introduced.

Scene screenshot sampling uses computed foreground/background source-over blends, not a per-glyph accessibility certification. The full black/white envelope bounds blur averaging and lighting variation for tested roles; actual painted swatches distinguish the live rendering. These checks do not prove every future background or physical Safari appearance. See [verification and limitations](verification.md).
