## Resulting interpretation

V3 used three filled mountain silhouettes, blob-like clouds and a compact lake in a **180px desktop/130px phone band**. V4 removes that band from the active Landscape. It uses a full-preview sky wash and three independently proportioned SVG planes: drawn sky/clouds, lightly filled mountain contours with sparse ridge lines, and open reflected contours/ripples extending to the bottom. Background washes have no hard horizon edge. Twenty static stars remain exclusive to Dark; the sun/moon has restrained translucent fill. Light/Dark share geometry, with decorative palette tokens rather than duplicate scene trees.

Far/mid/near contour opacity is 0.42/0.65/0.5, with 1–1.25px non-scaling strokes. Range fills fade from 20% to zero; the whole decorative atmosphere additionally follows the existing intensity control. Clouds have 12% fill, contour detail and bounded translation. Lake contours have 22% opacity and a faint reflected-light wash. There is no hatching, noise texture, filter turbulence, Canvas/WebGL or image download. Opaque cards and existing text plates preserve clarity; the glass reflects the same inherited light field.

The old treatment remains a running **Archive · V3 Landscape band** under Sources & archived comparisons, in either selected palette. The original V3 geometry and band dimensions are preserved, including its existing cloud/water drift and coordinated chrome. Archive selection is a temporary reference override, not a fourth environment. Returning to integrated themes preserves environment, palette, tuning and composition. All variants receive the corrected full-stage scene pulse; its original 70% truncation is intentionally not reproduced as a retained bug.

## Cue correction and scope

The old environment pulse had `height: 70%`; translation of a preview-sized layer also left edges uncovered while sweeping. The environment cue now covers the exact stage interior and pulses **in place**, without translation. Landscape sky/water pulses overlap vertically and reach the top/bottom respectively. Locally clipped chrome highlights retain their sweep. Brightness is bounded (scene pulse peaks at 0.65), no new target count or loops: five regular/seven landscape targets, one timer, 1,180ms maximum.

The full-page scene is tied to the preview's document height, not to a fixed screen or sticky/fixed wallpaper. Separate planes retain sensible proportions on tall phone compositions; water fills the lower space. Existing preview corner clipping is intentional containment, not a truncated cue. The outer tuning/document footer stays a neutral gallery shell.
